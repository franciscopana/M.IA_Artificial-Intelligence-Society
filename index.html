Individual

Profiling the dataset feature by feature with YData Profiling to find the issues that would quietly bias a model.

Algorithmic fairness · Car-insurance case study

04

Algorithmic Discrimination in Car Insurance

Group project

How insurers kept penalising Black neighbourhoods through proxy variables after Michigan's 2019 reform, plus a bias analysis of Allstate's rate data using disparate impact, regression residuals, census-linked subgroup analysis and SHAP.

Median premium rose with an area's share of Black residents, while SHAP showed no direct effect of race, gender or age, a pattern consistent with bias entering through geography.
